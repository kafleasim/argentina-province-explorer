What this supports: filling posts on time and keeping teachers in class. Whether a teacher is tenured or interim makes no visible difference to results; whether they turn up does. Statutes that wait more than 3 days for a substitute (9 of 18 provinces coded) leave classes uncovered.

How sure: suggestive only. The maths link rests on two provinces recoded on 17 Nov (Córdoba and La Pampa); without either recode it is no longer significant, and it does not survive adjustment for the 160 statute tests run. The stronger point is descriptive: the tools to assess quality (entry exams, results in ratings, principals choosing staff) are nearly absent, so there is no variation to test.

What this supports: recent subject training goes with slightly better maths (about 0.02 SD) in the 2025 census, but a long course shows nothing in ERCE 2019. Training reaches most teachers; the gap is that its content and its place in the career (pay, promotion, a classroom career path) are not set by the statutes.

Limits

  • Associations only: provinces, schools and pupils differ in ways we cannot fully hold constant.
  • With 24 provinces, a statute rule must go with a gap of about 13 points before we could detect it.
  • Statutes are not the whole rulebook: decrees and collective agreements also set these rules, and practice can differ.
  • Teacher answers are self-reports from those who replied; ERCE 2019 covers 167 state schools.
